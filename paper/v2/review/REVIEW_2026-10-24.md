## 2. Reviewer A (scientific)

**Question and framing.** The question, whether an NLA readout tells when
an answer has settled beyond cheaper signals, is clearly stated, and the
contributions credit prior work explicitly. Fixed: the introduction called
the model a "reasoning model"; it is an instruction-tuned model reasoning
step by step, and the framing now says so.

**Methods for the claims.** Appropriate: problem-level paired bootstrap,
problem-grouped cross-validation, fixed readings, pre-specified
sensitivity analyses. Fixed: the tail's three criteria were never stated
(§3.2); Design A was labelled registered although it was introduced post
hoc on the main run (D69) and registered only for the replication (D75).

**Fairness of comparisons.** H1's comparator is the registered correctness
probe, the weaker probe; the paper says so and reports the same-label probe
separately. The confirmatory comparison uses the outcome-selected menu, and
the paper reports that as the reason the advantage cannot be attributed to
content.

**Interpretation.** Warranted after the fixes below. Two substantive
attribution errors were found by checking numbers against the stored
outputs rather than against the decision record (§3).

**Limitations.** Explicit. Added: multiplicity outside the pre-specified
families, and possible benchmark contamination.

**Contradictions.** None remain that the checks can detect.
