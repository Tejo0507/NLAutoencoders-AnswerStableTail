# References

The report's bibliography. Numbering and entries are those of
`Project_Review_II.md` §References, the submitted review the `[n]` citations
were written against, so every citation keeps the number it had there. It is
kept here, in the repository, so that the report can be assembled from
version-controlled files alone.

**Checked 2026-10-19.** Every entry was compared with its source:

- the 23 with an arXiv record by title and ordered author surnames against
  the arXiv API;
- [2] and [21] against Crossref (title, volume, pages, year);
- [10], [23] and [26] against their publishing pages.

Every in-text attribution flagged for verification in the drafts was checked
against the cited paper's abstract or body. Two entries differed from their
source and are corrected below; the review's copy is unchanged.

- **[26]** omitted four of its 26 authors (A. Tamkin, E. Durmus, T. Hume and
  F. Mosconi, between M. MacDiarmid and C. D. Freeman).
- **[27]** gave an author list that omitted K. Hermann and misplaced two
  authors; it follows arXiv:2303.17651 now.

Venue names and page ranges were not checked against the proceedings, except
for the two DOIs.
