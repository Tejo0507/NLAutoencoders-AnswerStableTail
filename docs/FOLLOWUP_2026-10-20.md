## 3. Position-only control and truncation audit, CPU only (D79)

Plan frozen before any result: `docs/POSITION_TRUNCATION_PLAN.md`. Results:
`docs/POSITION_TRUNCATION_RESULTS.md`. Exploratory on `main` and
`main_nla240`; pre-specified and secondary for `rep1`.

- **A rule with no readout reproduces the readout's design-A advantage over
  no signal.** The rule is "stop at the first menu chunk if it comes within
  T tokens" (post hoc here). The registered estimand rewards a few very
  early, mostly unsafe stops plus abstention.
- **Every advantage disappears if a cut-off forced completion is read as the
  answer the model's own continuations reach.** Before the tail start, 54–57%
  of forced completions are cut off at 48 tokens. Of those, 98–99% disagree
  with the continuations' majority, which equals the final answer 96–98% of
  the time.
