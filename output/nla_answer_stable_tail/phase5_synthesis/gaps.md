# Gaps and falsifiers

- No evidence yet that a NLA can distinguish redundant from productive verification on independently labeled traces.
- NLA checkpoint compatibility is model/layer exact; it cannot directly decode arbitrary model activations.
- RECAP cannot be retrofitted by merely training a post hoc probe. It requires co-training specified content into the target representations.
- Answer stability, text repetition, and low entropy are correlated signals with different failure modes.
- Any claim of an "internal semantic state responsible for" a tail requires causal manipulation that changes verification behavior and compute/accuracy in the predicted direction.
