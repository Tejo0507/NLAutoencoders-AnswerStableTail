# Full-text selection

Eight works were selected because together they test the proposed inference chain: NLA readout → representation evidence → answer stability → redundancy → causal usefulness.

1. Fraser-Taliente et al. 2026 — method, source limitations, released checkpoints.
2. Dingeto 2026 — direct challenge to reconstruction-based faithfulness.
3. Liu & Wang 2025 — closest answer-convergence baseline.
4. Zhang et al. 2025 — hidden-state correctness-probe baseline.
5. Caldarella et al. 2026 — post-correctness/harmful-overthinking operationalization.
6. Farquhar et al. 2024 — semantic versus lexical uncertainty.
7. Huang et al. 2024 — limits of intrinsic self-correction.
8. Goldowsky-Dill et al. 2023 — controls required for activation patching.
